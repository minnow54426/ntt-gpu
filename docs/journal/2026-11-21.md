# Analyze Ada timings and prepare conversion measurements

## Outcome

The raw November 19 archive is available on the M1 Pro and its checksum
matches. The earlier cloud workspace's missing-input blocker is resolved
here. Revalidated the paired measurements and balanced repeats, then wrote
the [analysis](../paired-ntt-native-analysis.md).

Group twiddles improved all eight device-event NTT workloads. Median
legacy/group ratios were 1.515 for forward, 1.503 for inverse and 1.493 for
fused multiplication. Resident-interface repeats showed substantially
smaller differences. P2/D11 remains open.

## Reasoning

The source of a speedup matters when selecting the next optimization.
Host public calls, the resident interface and device events measure different
work. Whole-profile stream creation includes setup excluded by the benchmark.
These distinctions make conversion parameter setup, allocation and transfer
costs useful targets for the next measurements.

covers six scopes, special-prime metadata and sample admission. The focused

rejected by the native collector. This preparation ran locally.
