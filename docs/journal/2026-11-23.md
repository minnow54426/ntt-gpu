# Give every notebook page a clear reading structure

## Outcome

Pulled both repositories before reviewing the complete notebook. The incoming
changes include the paired Ada analysis, resident conversion benchmarks,
optimization research and repeatable engineering procedures. The timing
analysis is complete; the next technical deliverable is the prepared RTX 5090

## Reasoning

The shared renderer hid every article body and deliberately removed its
section navigation. Readers could see a summary but could not scan the
explanation or choose a section. The broad Learn and Research groups also
mixed introductory material, backend contracts and measured results.

## Changes

Reviewed the purpose, main headings and reading dependencies of every source
article, together with the dated journal structure. Grouped the directory and
left navigation into Start here, Fundamentals, CUDA runtime, OpenFHE, Results,
Research, Procedures, Plans, Daily journal and Contribute. Each article now
starts with its purpose, key points and prerequisites, then its detailed
content and a suggested next page. Guides and interactive lessons open by
default; dated journal records remain collapsible.

Restored a section sidebar on every page, including a phone Sections menu.
Section links open enclosing notes and preserve existing bookmarks. Removed
the duplicate source title from rendered articles while retaining its anchor.
Added main headings to the short procedures index. Kept the existing theme,
mathematics, source citations, interactive examples and measured records.
Updated the home page's next-step explanation to reflect the incoming analysis
and the prepared resident conversion chain.
